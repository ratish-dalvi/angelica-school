# Angelica's Writing Style

This guide is sent to the AI with every letter. Edit it whenever your style changes; plain language works best.

## Tone
- Warm, personal, and genuinely caring, but natural and sincere. Not flowery, cheesy, or over the top.
- Keep it positive and realistic. It shouldn't read like a string of compliments.
- Balance professional language with personal warmth.
- Balance academic achievements with personal character.
- Write with conviction, and use varied vocabulary.

## Structure
1. School header
2. Opening, e.g. "It is my great pleasure to write this letter of recommendation on behalf of [Student Name]"
3. How I met the student (usually in my Global History class)
4. Academic qualities and specific achievements
5. Personal characteristics and social qualities
6. Future goals and why the student is well suited to them
7. Strong concluding recommendation
8. Offer to provide more information, then signature

## What to include
- Specific details, examples, and anecdotes that show I know the student well
- How long I've known the student
- The student's specific interests and career goals
- Family background and personal circumstances, when relevant

## Phrases I often use
Vary the wording. Don't copy these word for word every time.
- "I first met [student] in September 2022 when [he/she] was a student in my Global History class"
- "I have no doubt that [student] will bring the same [quality] to the collegiate environment"
- "characterized by [his/her] extraordinary work ethic"
- "intelligent, thoughtful, and naturally curious"
- "highly respected by [his/her] peers"
- "authentically [himself/herself]"
- "It has been wonderful to get to know [student] over the past [time period]"
- "any university will be lucky to have [him/her] as a student"

## Closing
End with: "Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions or require any further information."
